Balloon

Fifteen balloons. Each pump adds 50p to the balloon's pot, and you can collect it whenever you like, but every balloon has a hidden burst point, and a burst loses that balloon's pot. It measures how much risk you take for a reward, a task type used in some personality and behavioural assessments. It doesn't build finance knowledge.

Play

How to play

Press Pump to add 50p to the balloon. Press Collect to bank the pot and move to the next balloon. If the balloon bursts, you lose that balloon's pot but nothing you've already banked. Each balloon bursts at its own random point, and nothing tells you where.

Scoring

Your score is the total you bank across the balloons. The run ends after the fifteenth balloon or at two minutes, whichever comes first, and the balloon in play when time runs out is lost.

Best practice

  • There's no right level of risk, only a trade-off: each extra pump adds 50p, but raises the chance of losing everything in that balloon.
  • Pick a stopping habit and test it over several balloons, instead of deciding afresh each time. One burst or one lucky balloon tells you very little.
  • After each run, look at the burst points in the results. They show how the risk is spread, and whether a low score was a cautious style or just bad luck.
  • Don't chase. A burst doesn't make the next balloon safer: every balloon is independent.
