Areas of Finance Cheat Sheets Recruitment Calendar
The recruiting year at a glance
When applications typically open and close for each programme type. Exact dates shift firm to firm and year to year — treat this as the shape of the year, not a fixed deadline list.
The key thing non-targets often miss: applications open months before the programme itself runs — often 6–10 months ahead. Autumn/winter applications are for a programme that happens the following spring or summer. Miss the autumn window and that route is closed for a full year, not just a few weeks.
