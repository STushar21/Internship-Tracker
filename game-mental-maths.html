Mental maths

Two minutes of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. The next question appears the moment you type the right answer. It measures the arithmetic speed that numerical tests and some interviews check.

Play

How to play

Type the answer. There's no Enter: when it's right, the next question appears. If you type a wrong digit, backspace and carry on. There's no penalty except the time, and no skipping.

Scoring

One point for each correct answer in 120 seconds. Addition and subtraction use numbers from 2 to 100. Multiplication and division use 2 to 12 against 2 to 100. Subtraction and division are built backwards, so every answer is a whole, positive number.

Best practice

  • Add left to right: for 47 + 68, do 47 + 60 = 107, then + 8 = 115.
  • Split multiplication: for 14 × 37, do 10 × 37 + 4 × 37.
  • For division, ask what times the small number gives the big one.
  • Don't type a digit you aren't sure of. A backspace costs more time than a slightly slower answer. Use the same method for each type of question until it's automatic.
