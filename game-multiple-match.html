Multiple Match

Forty-five seconds of sector benchmarks. Each card shows a sector, a metric and a value, and you call it High or Low against that sector's typical range. It builds the sense of what's normal for a sector, which valuation questions in interviews assume.

Play

How to play

A card shows a sector, a metric and a value, such as 'Cybersecurity, EV/Revenue 4.1x'. Tap High if the value is above that sector's typical range and Low if it's below. The value is always clearly outside the range. You have about 3.5 seconds per card, and after each one the typical range appears briefly at the bottom.

Scoring

Each correct call scores points equal to your combo multiplier: ×1 to start, ×2 after three in a row, ×3 after six and ×4 after nine. A wrong call or a timeout resets the combo and costs 2 seconds.

Best practice

  • Place the sector first: recurring-revenue, high-margin sectors sit at the top, and cyclical, capital-heavy ones sit at the bottom.
  • Check the metric. Banks and insurers are valued on P/TBV, P/B and P/E, and REITs on P/FFO and price to NAV. EV/EBITDA doesn't apply to them.
  • Learn the reason on each card, not only the number. 'Banks trade near book value' carries over to sectors the deck doesn't cover.
  • These are rough through-the-cycle ranges for large and mid-cap listed companies, for building intuition. Real multiples move with rates and the cycle, and individual companies can sit well outside them.
