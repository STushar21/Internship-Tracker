How this is calculated

Each set with a weight above 0 kg and 1 to 12 reps gets an estimated one-rep max: the weight itself for 1 rep, otherwise weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30) (the Epley formula), rounded to 0.1 kg.

A session's value is its best set's estimate. Sets above 12 reps and sets without a weight are left out of the estimate, because the formula isn't reliable there.

A session is a new best when its value is higher than every earlier session's; the first session isn't counted as one. If an exercise has no weighted sets at all, its sessions are compared by their most reps instead.

The heaviest set uses the Gym page's "New best" rule (the heaviest weight, then the most reps, at any rep count), so it can differ from the best estimate.
